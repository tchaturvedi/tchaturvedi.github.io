## What this is

`chamberlain` is the flagship of this portfolio: a control plane that removes the
human gatekeeper from shipping a workload — the same problem solved, in different
forms, across 14 years of platform work — while making the multi-tenant guarantees
(isolation, authorization, credential scoping, safe rollout) provable rather than
assumed.

## Honest status, as of this page

Nothing is running yet. This page exists before the code does, on purpose — see the
